# A9 — The spring training label on Standings (S129)

Amends the ratified [standings-search-settings design](../DESIGN.md) (ratified 2026-10-14) and its amendments [A1](A1.md)–[A8](A8.md). Story **S129**; built by S130 (from S128's `phase`). **Draft, not ratified.**

**Adds:** a label under the Standings page title saying which table the page holds: during spring training (S128's `phase: "spring"`), "Spring training 2027 · as of Mar 2"; and, pending Q2, for the off-season final table (`phase: "final"`), "2026 regular season · final".
**Overrides:** the page title in `standings-mobile`, `standings-medium` and `standings-desktop`, which becomes a heading block: the title, then the label line under it. Only while the page holds a spring (or, pending Q2, a final) table; the regular season's page stays as drawn, with no label.
**Repeals:** nothing.

## Decisions

Recorded as the owner stated them.

- Spring training standings are shown and labeled (owner, 2026-11-07, asked what to show from the first spring game until opening day, with spring training standings, labeled as such, suggested): "Agreed".
- The off-season shows the prior season's final table (owner, 2026-11-07): "Yes, it should always show the last standings for the prior season until spring training’s first game".

## Interpretation

- *Agent:* the label is a muted meta line under the title (VISUAL-DESIGN §4: the page title, then a `sm`, muted meta line), not a badge or a coloured banner: red is brand and strikes (§3), and nothing here is an alert. The title stays `Standings` so the page keeps the name the nav rail gives it.
- *Agent:* the label reads "Spring training 2027 · as of Mar 2": "spring training" in plain words, the season, and the day the table counts to, so a reader in March knows the records are spring games, not the season's.
- *Agent:* the drawn records (`18`–`9` and so on) are illustrative spring numbers; the final frames' are illustrative full-season numbers.

## Open questions

1. **Q1.** **Is a muted line under the title, "Spring training 2027 · as of Mar 2", the label you want for spring training standings?** That is how it is drawn: the title stays `Standings` and a quieter line under it names the table. Alternatives are a small badge beside the title, or retitling the page "Spring training standings".
2. **Q2.** **Should the off-season final table be labeled too, "2026 regular season · final"?** From the end of the regular season until spring training's first game, Standings shows the last season's final table. If yes, the `standings-final-*` frames stay and FEATURES records your words; if no, those frames are dropped and the final table stays as the ratified frames draw it, with no label.
