# A8 — Settings in two framed columns, the install tip on top (S73)

Amends the ratified [standings-search-settings design](../DESIGN.md) (ratified 2026-10-14) and its [A1](A1.md)–[A7](A7.md). Story **S73** (it builds the App row these changes move). A written amendment: the owner's directions at S73's design match, ratified as words (no Figma frames). The S73 captures that match it become its drawn record.

**Adds:** a frame (a bordered panel) around each of two groups, **Settings** (Game List, Game Detail) and **Account**; a small "tip" mark on the install message; a way to dismiss that message for good in this browser.
**Overrides:** `settings-*` and A1's `settings-install-mobile` / `-desktop` and A2's `settings-install-ios-mobile`: on medium and desktop the two columns are separated by purpose, Settings on one side and Account on the other, each in its own frame. Within the Settings frame, Game List and Game Detail are more distinct sections. The App group (the install link, or the iPhone/iPad Share steps) moves to the very top of the page, outside both frames, as a tip with a small tip indicator.
**Repeals:** A1's App group placed above Account inside the right-hand column.

## Frames

None. The original frames stand for the controls themselves; the captures at S73's design match show the new layout.

## Decisions

Recorded as the owner stated them, on 2026-11-02, at S73's design match: "The settings page's 2 column layout is really odd as-is. The columns should be separated by purpose (Settings and Account) with frames around them for visual separation, and with the sections more distinct within each (Game List vs Game Detail, etc). The App section should be at the very top outside of those frames with a little "tip" indicator, and it should be dismissable at a browser level via localStorage. Otherwise it looks good."

## Interpretation

- *Agent:* on mobile (one column) the order is the tip, then the Settings frame, then the Account frame.
- *Agent:* dismissing the tip is remembered in this browser only, through `web/src/storage/` under a key labeled `none` (ADR-028: it says nothing about the person), and it never syncs to the account.
