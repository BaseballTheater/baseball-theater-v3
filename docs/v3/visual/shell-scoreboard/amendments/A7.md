# A7 — The Win Probability slot on live and final games (S107)

Amends the ratified [shell-scoreboard design](../DESIGN.md) (ratified 2026-10-14) and its amendments [A1](A1.md)–[A6](A6.md). Story **S107**; built by S108. **Draft, not ratified.**

**Adds:** the Win Probability slot occupant, when the reader's Game-Row Slot is Win Probability, on a live row (the line so far and both teams' current chance) and a final row (the whole game's line); on a scheduled row it reads "Odds at first pitch"; with Hide Scores on, a live or final row's occupant reads "–" and "Hidden with scores".
**Overrides:** nothing.
**Repeals:** the pre-game win probability bar on the scheduled row (DESIGN.md: "win probability (scheduled row)"): no feed gives pre-game odds.

Source: https://www.figma.com/design/wqGKEFlvyxq0ObWZhRJdPF — section "W — Win Probability slot (S107, awaiting ratification)", live file as edited in the S107 session (no named version).

## Decisions

Recorded as the owner stated them. On 2026-10-30, told that the frames draw win probability only before first pitch and no feed gives pre-game odds, the owner said: "It is actually least useful before the game. During and after is definitely useful."

- Win probability shows during and after the game, from S93's play metrics (`winProbabilitySeries`, S106).
- The pre-game bar is repealed.

## Choices made while drawing (to accept or change at ratification)

- The occupant keeps the slot's size and border; the line, area, dashed 50% line and Accent value are the Live room's Win probability card (S106) in small.
- The line is the home team's chance, as in the Live room's card; the value at top right names the home team.
- A final row shows no percentage (it would read 100% or 0%), only the line and `Final`.

## Open questions

1. With Hide Scores on, a live or final row's odds would give the score away. Is `–` and `Hidden with scores` right, or should the slot show something else there (the count, the pitchers, or nothing)?
2. On a scheduled row, is `Odds at first pitch` right, or should the slot fall back to another occupant before the game?
3. With the pre-game bar repealed, the default Status slot's scheduled row loses its drawn occupant. Should it show nothing there, or something else?
