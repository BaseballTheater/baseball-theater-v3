# A6 — The update notice floats at the bottom, with Snooze and Update Now (S28, S73)

Amends the ratified [shell-scoreboard design](../DESIGN.md) (ratified 2026-10-14) and its [A1](A1.md)–[A5](A5.md). Stories **S28** (a retired version's prompt) and **S73** (a waiting new version). A written amendment: the owner's directions at S28's design match, ratified as words (no Figma frames). The S28 and S73 captures that match it become its drawn record.

**Adds:** Snooze, which hides the notice for one hour, after which it comes back.
**Overrides:** `shell-notice-mobile` / `-medium` / `-desktop` and A1's `shell-update-mobile` / `-medium` / `-desktop`: the "reload to update" notice is a floating, notification-style box fixed at the bottom of the screen, not a banner in the slot under the header. Its message sits on top; underneath, two buttons aligned right: **Snooze** and **Update Now**.
**Repeals:** the ✕ dismiss and the `Reload` text link on that notice, and its place under the header.

## Frames

None. The captures at S28's and S73's design matches show it.

## Decisions

Recorded as the owner stated them, on 2026-11-02, at S28's design match: "The reload notice should be a floating notification-style box at the bottom, fixed. Replace the X with Snooze, and make it an hour-long snooze before it comes back. Change Reload to Update Now. Make both actions right-aligned buttons underneath the description rather than floated to the right."

## Interpretation

- *Agent:* this is the one "reload to update" notice, so it covers both S28's retired-version prompt and S73's waiting-worker notice (one component, ADR-025). A1's offline marker (S74) isn't a reload notice and keeps its place under the header, with no actions.
- *Agent:* Update Now is the primary button and Snooze the secondary one, Snooze first so Update Now sits at the far right.
- *Agent:* a snooze is remembered in this browser (through `web/src/storage/`, a key labeled `none`), so a reload within the hour doesn't bring the notice straight back.
