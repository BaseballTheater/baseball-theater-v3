# A9 — The changelist page, and what's new on the update notices (S133)

Amends the ratified [shell-scoreboard design](../DESIGN.md) (ratified 2026-10-14) and its ratified amendments [A1](A1.md)–[A6](A6.md). Story **S133**; built by S134 (the page) and S135 (the notices and the version line). **Draft, not ratified.**

**Adds:** a page that lists every release's approved notes (S131's `ReleaseNotes`, served by S132's `GET /api/v1/releases`), newest first, in S131's three buckets: the headline additions, each other item labeled by its kind, and the one "fixes and polish" line; with its empty, loading and error states. A "See what's new" link and the big additions on A6's update-available notice. An after-update notification that floats where A6 does, says which release the app now runs, shows the big additions of the releases since the one last seen in this browser, with the link, and dismisses itself.
**Overrides:** A6's floating notice (written in words; the S28 and S73 captures are its record): a link sits at the left of its button row, and the big additions sit between its message and its buttons. The version line at the foot of the rail in A5's `scoreboard-header-version-desktop`, A2's `shell-version-desktop` and `shell-signed-out-desktop`, and in the drawer in A2's `nav-drawer-mobile`, `-medium`, reads the build's release version once the build knows it.
**Repeals:** nothing.

In words, not drawn:

- **The version line.** The drawer on mobile and medium shows the same line as the rail. Since #47 (2026-11-13) every build already prints its release, the milestone of the pull request whose merge commit it builds, with the commit (`v3.2.0 (40767e9)`); a build with none prints `unreleased (<commit>)` or `dev (<commit>)` and counts as a null release for S135 AC 3, so it records nothing and shows no after-update pointer; the update-available notice doesn't depend on the release and is unaffected. Pending Q2: the version line, in the rail and the drawer, is also a link to the page; it looks as drawn (no underline until hovered or focused).
- **Releases on the page.** A release with no date shows its version alone; one with only fixes and polish shows its version, its date if it has one, and that line. Every release is listed on one page, with no pagination (the list starts at v3.1.0). Long titles and sentences wrap; nothing on the page is cut. Release notes carry no scores, so Hide Scores changes nothing on the page or the notices.
- **When the after-update notification shows** (S135 AC 3). Every load of a build whose release is known records that release in this browser, under its own storage key (never A6's `bt.updateSnoozedUntil`), so a reload doesn't show it again. It shows only on a load whose release is newer (`compareReleaseVersions`) than the recorded one. Nothing shows on a first visit (nothing recorded), on the same release (a new commit of it included), on an older one (a rollback), when the build's release is unknown, or when storage throws; a recorded value that isn't a release version counts as nothing recorded and is overwritten.
- **Which additions the after-update notification lists** (S135 AC 3, "the releases in between"): the headline additions of every release after the recorded one, up to and including the running build's release. S132's `?after=` stops at the server's own release, which can be newer than the running build (the `updated-notice-with-update-*` case), so S135 drops any newer release with `compareReleaseVersions`. When the request fails or returns none, the notification shows its message and link alone (`updated-notice-*`), as A6 shows its link alone (`update-notice-link-*`).
- **Counting what doesn't fit.** Pending Q6: exactly three additions show two and "+1 more big addition" (singular); more show two and "+N more big additions". On a notice, a title wraps and a sentence stops after two lines with "…" (a choice ratified with the amendment).
- **On the page itself.** Neither notice shows its link while the reader is already on the page (a choice ratified with the amendment).
- **Touch screens.** Pending Q3: there is no hover, so the countdown pauses only while focus is inside the notification; the link and `✕` act at once.
- **All three at once.** Pending Q5: the marker stays under the header, A6 sits at the bottom and the after-update notification above it, as the two `updated-notice-with-*` frame sets draw each pair.
- **While additions load.** Pending Q11: a notice appears at once with its message and link; its additions fill in when they arrive, the box growing upward since it is anchored at the bottom. The after-update notification's countdown starts once the additions settle (arrived, failed or none), after 3 seconds at most.
- **The retired case** is not drawn with the after-update notification: that notification appears only on a fresh load of a newer build, which is not retired.

## Open questions

1. **Q1.** **Should the page live at `/whats-new` and be called "What's new"?** That is how it is drawn. The alternative is `/changelist` (or another address) and the title "Changelist".
2. **Q2.** **Should the page be reached only from the notices' links and the version line at the foot of the rail and drawer?** As drawn, it isn't in the nav list; tapping the version line opens it. Alternatives: add it to the nav list, or put a link in Settings.
3. **Q3.** **Should the after-update notification dismiss itself after 10 seconds?** The countdown pauses while the pointer is over it or focus is inside it (on a touch screen, only while focus is inside), and the `✕` closes it at once. Any other number of seconds works the same way.
4. **Q4.** **When the server has retired this version, should the update notice hide its link and show no additions?** That build can't load the notes (every route of its version answers 426), so its link would open the page's error state. As drawn, it shows only the message, Snooze and Update Now; after updating, the after-update notification gives the link. The alternative is to keep a link that runs Update Now first and opens the page once the new version has loaded.
5. **Q5.** **When the update notice is also up, should the after-update notification stack directly above it at the bottom?** As drawn, the update notice keeps the bottom and the after-update notification sits 8px above; the offline marker under the header stays where it is. The alternative is to show one at a time (the after-update notification first, the update notice after it dismisses).
6. **Q6.** **Should a notice show at most two big additions, then "+N more big additions"?** That keeps the box short on a phone, and exactly three reads "+1 more big addition". The alternative is a different cap, or every addition.
7. **Q7.** **Should the rail's version line keep the commit after the release version, `v3.2.0 (c46e853)`?** You already decided it shows the release version (Decisions); this asks only whether the commit stays beside it, so two builds of one release can be told apart. The alternative is `v3.2.0` alone.
8. **Q8.** **Should the kind labels on the page read FEATURE, SETTING, FIX and DESIGN, all in one neutral style?** Each item that gets its own entry carries one of S131's four kinds as a small uppercase label. The alternatives are plain words ("New feature", "New setting", "Fix", "Design change"), or a different colour for fixes.
9. **Q9.** **Should the page's footer read only "baseball.theater"?** Other pages' footers say how fresh their data is ("Fetched 12s ago · baseball.theater"), but release notes are fixed when a version is built and the releases request carries no fetch time, so the redrawn page frames show `baseball.theater` alone. The alternative keeps the usual footer with a fetch age from the request itself.
10. **Q10.** **When both notices are up and both have big additions, should only the update notice show them, with the after-update notification showing its message and link alone?** Shown in full, the two boxes would cover about half a phone screen. The `updated-notice-with-update-*` frames draw the pair with no additions; with yes, the update notice keeps its additions there and the after-update notification shows its message and link alone. The alternatives are both in full, or neither showing additions while both are up.
11. **Q11.** **While a notice's big additions load, should the notice appear at once and grow upward when they arrive, with the after-update countdown starting once they settle (3 seconds at most)?** The additions come from a request made when the notice is raised. The alternative is to wait for the request (3 seconds at most) and show the notice complete, with its countdown starting then.
