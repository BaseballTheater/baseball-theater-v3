# A8 — The scoreboard's calendar date picker (S123)

Amends the ratified [shell-scoreboard design](../DESIGN.md) (ratified 2026-10-14) and its amendments [A1](A1.md)–[A7](A7.md). Story **S123**; built by S125 (counts from S124). **Draft, not ratified.**

**Adds:** a calendar that opens from the scoreboard's date: a date field the user can type into (and its invalid state), a month grid with its weekday row and each day's game count, days with no games dimmed but pickable, year and decade views for switching the year, a month still loading its counts, and an off-season month. On mobile it opens as a bottom sheet; on medium and desktop as a popover under the date.
**Overrides:** the date control's label (DESIGN.md, `scoreboard-*` frames: ‹ `SEP 21, 2026` ›) becomes a button: a calendar glyph, the date, and a `▾`, inside a hairline outline. The previous / next-day arrows stay.
**Repeals:** nothing.

Source: https://www.figma.com/design/wqGKEFlvyxq0ObWZhRJdPF — section "X — Calendar date picker (S123, awaiting ratification)", live file as edited in the S123 session (no named version).

## Components

What S125 builds from Mantine's own date components (`@mantine/dates`, Mantine 7; S125 adds the package beside `@mantine/core` and its `dayjs` peer) and what is custom, so nothing is designed in code.

- **Mantine `DatePicker`** (inline, not the input-plus-dropdown `DatePickerInput`): the month grid, its weekday row, the month header with ‹ › and the level button, and the switch between its three levels — `month` (days), `year` (months) and `decade` (years) — via `level` / `onLevelChange`. `firstDayOfWeek={0}` gives the drawn `SU`-first weekday row; `weekdayFormat="dd"` uppercased by style gives `SU MO …`.
- **`renderDay`** on that `DatePicker`: draws each day as its number over its game count (muted, 11px), or the loading bar while the month's counts load. **`getDayProps`** sets the dimmed style (35% opacity) on a day with zero games, without `disabled`, so it stays pickable.
- **`getMonthControlProps`** on the year level: dims months with no games and adds each month's count under its name (the custom part is the two-line label).
- **`minDate={new Date(1901, 3, 18)}`** and no `maxDate`: nothing before 1901-04-18 is pickable (Mantine disables those days, months and years); future dates are pickable.
- **Mantine `DateInput`**: the date field the user types into (`valueFormat="MMM D, YYYY"`, `dateParser` accepting typed forms such as `9/21/2026` and `2026-09-21`), with its `error` prop drawing the invalid state (Primary Light outline and message). Its own dropdown calendar is suppressed (S125 chooses how) so the one calendar on screen is the `DatePicker` below it, kept in step with the field.
- **Mantine `Popover`** (medium, desktop) and **`Drawer`** with `position="bottom"` (mobile) hold the panel; the grab handle, scrim colour and corner radii are styled on them.
- **Custom:** the date button (calendar glyph, date, `▾`, hairline outline, Raised current while open) that replaces the plain label in `DateControl.tsx`; the per-day and per-month game count text and its loading bar; the "Counting games…" and "No games this month." lines; the `Today` / `Close` footer; and the theme styles mapping Mantine's selected day to Primary and the panel to `color/bg/raised`.

## Decisions

Recorded as the owner stated them.

- The calendar (owner, 2026-11-07): "I also want a calendar picker UI that makes it easy to switch the year, lets you pick with the calendar visible and the day of the week showing in the calendar grid, and the date display should let you type the date in yourself. If we could also show the number of games on each day in that view that would be great."
- No-game days (owner, 2026-11-07, asked whether they are dimmed): "dimmed". They stay pickable.
- Future dates (owner, 2026-11-07, asked whether they are allowed): "allow future".
- Nothing before 1901-04-18 is pickable — the story's bound (S123 AC 1).

## Choices made while drawing (to accept or change at ratification)

- *Agent:* mobile opens a bottom sheet (full width, grab handle, 70% Dark scrim); medium and desktop open a 344px popover under the date button with no scrim. The page behind stays drawn so the reader keeps their place.
- *Agent:* the date field sits above the grid, labelled `DATE`, with a muted hint "Type a date, or pick one below."; an invalid date shows "Not a date. Type it like Sep 21, 2026." and does not navigate; a valid typed date navigates once it parses (on Enter or blur).
- *Agent:* the count is a bare number under the day (`15`), muted, with no "games" word, to fit seven columns at 390px; the year view says `391 games` because it has room.
- *Agent:* dimming is 35% opacity of the whole day, the same for a day with no games and every day of an off-season month; a loading month dims nothing.
- *Agent:* the selected day keeps Primary as its fill (filled red, per the "Red on Dark" decision) with Light text.
- *Agent:* the footer's `Today` jumps to today's date and closes; `Close` closes without changing the date (tapping the scrim or pressing Escape does the same).
- *Agent:* the drawn counts are illustrative (a 15-game day, 9 or 10 on Mondays and Thursdays, `4` on Wild Card days); real counts come from S124.
