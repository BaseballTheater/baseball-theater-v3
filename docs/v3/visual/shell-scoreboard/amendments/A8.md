# A8 — The scoreboard's calendar date picker (S123)

Amends the ratified [shell-scoreboard design](../DESIGN.md) (ratified 2026-10-14) and its amendments [A1](A1.md)–[A7](A7.md). Story **S123**; built by S125 (counts from S124). **Draft, not ratified.**

**Adds:** a calendar that opens from the scoreboard's date: a date field the user can type into (and its invalid states: not a date, and a date before 1901-04-18), a month grid with its weekday row and each day's game count, days with no games dimmed but pickable, year and decade views for switching the year, a month still loading its counts, a month whose counts failed to load, an off-season month, and the earliest bound (nothing before 1901-04-18 can be picked). On mobile it opens as a bottom sheet; on medium and desktop as a popover under the date.
**Overrides:** the date control's label in `scoreboard-mobile`, `-medium`, `-desktop` (and in every other ratified frame that draws it, ‹ `SEP 21, 2026` ›) becomes a button: a calendar glyph, the date, and a `▾`, inside a hairline outline. The previous / next-day arrows stay.
**Repeals:** nothing.

Source: https://www.figma.com/design/wqGKEFlvyxq0ObWZhRJdPF — section "X — Calendar date picker (S123, awaiting ratification)", live file as edited in the S123 session (no named version).

## Components

What S125 builds from Mantine's own date components (`@mantine/dates`, Mantine 7; S125 adds the package beside `@mantine/core` and its `dayjs` peer) and what is custom, so nothing is designed in code. Behaviour named here was checked against `@mantine/dates` 7.17.8's source.

- **Mantine `DatePicker`** (inline, not the input-plus-dropdown `DatePickerInput`): the month grid, its weekday row, the month header with ‹ › and the level button, and the switch between its three levels — `month` (days), `year` (months) and `decade` (years) — via `level` / `onLevelChange`. `firstDayOfWeek={0}` gives the drawn `SU`-first weekday row; `weekdayFormat="dd"` uppercased by style gives `SU MO …`. `hideOutsideDates` leaves the cells before the 1st and after the last day empty, as every month frame draws them: S124 answers one month per request, so a neighbouring month's days would have no count and, dimmed as Mantine draws them, would look like days with no games. At the `decade` level Mantine draws the header title as plain text, not a button (`DecadeLevel` passes `hasNextLevel: false`), which is why the decade frames show no `▾`.
- **`renderDay`** on that `DatePicker`: draws each day as its number over its game count (muted, 11px), or the loading bar while the month's counts load. **`getDayProps`** sets the dimmed style (35% opacity) on a day with no games, without `disabled`, so it stays pickable. `DatePicker` marks the selected day itself; it passes `selected` only to days.
- **`getMonthControlProps`** on the year level and **`getYearControlProps`** on the decade level: `DatePicker` never marks a month or a year `selected` itself (its own selection reaches only `getDayProps`), so S125 passes `selected` for the month in the same month and year as the date (`SEP` in the year frames) and for the year of the date (`2026` in the decade frames). `getMonthControlProps` also dims months with no games, without `disabled`. It can't replace a month's label: Mantine 7.17's `MonthsList` spreads those props and then sets the formatted month name as the control's children, and offers no render prop for a month. So the count line under the name is custom, added without replacing the name (for example a `data-count` attribute drawn with `::after`). This part is pending Q5.
- **`minDate={new Date(1901, 3, 18)}`** on the `DatePicker`, and no `maxDate`: Mantine marks every day, month and year before 1901-04-18 `disabled` and the ‹ control at the bound disabled, styled as the earliest frames draw them (20%, no count, not focusable) so they read apart from a dimmed no-game day (35%, pickable). Future dates are pickable.
- **Mantine `DateInput`**: the date field the user types into (`valueFormat="MMM D, YYYY"`), with its `error` prop drawing the invalid states (Primary Light outline and message). Its own dropdown calendar is turned off with `popoverProps={{ disabled: true }}`, so the one calendar on screen is the `DatePicker` below it, kept in step with the field. How S125 wires it, because DateInput's defaults fight the drawn states:
  - One strict `dateParser` reads typed forms such as `Sep 21, 2026`, `9/21/2026` and `2026-09-21`. A typed date counts only if it formats back to the same year, month and day, so `Feb 30, 2026` is "Not a date", not March 2.
  - S125 does not navigate from DateInput's `onChange`, which fires on every keystroke that parses (`9/2` on the way to `9/21` is September 2). On Enter, or when the field loses focus, it parses the field's text with that parser and either navigates or sets `error`.
  - `fixOnBlur={false}`, so leaving the field keeps the typed text and its error instead of quietly restoring the last valid date.
  - No `minDate` on the `DateInput`: with one, Mantine silently ignores a parsed date before the bound and shows no error. The parser checks the bound and shows the "Games start Apr 18, 1901." error (the `tooearly` frames).
- **Mantine `Popover`** (medium, desktop) and **`Drawer`** with `position="bottom"` (mobile) hold the panel; the grab handle, scrim colour and corner radii are styled on them.
- **Custom:** the date button (calendar glyph, date, `▾`, hairline outline, Raised current while open) that replaces the plain label in `DateControl.tsx`; the `▾` after the month header's `September 2026` and the year header's `2026` (Mantine's level button draws only its label, with no chevron; S125 adds it, for example through a `monthLabelFormat` / `yearLabelFormat` function or CSS `::after`); the per-day and per-month game count text and its loading bar; the "Counting games…", "Couldn't load game counts. Days are still pickable.", "No games this month." and "Games start Apr 18, 1901." lines; the `Today` / `Close` footer; and the theme styles mapping Mantine's selected day to Primary, disabled days to 20%, and the panel to `color/bg/raised`.

## Decisions

Recorded as the owner stated them.

- The calendar (owner, 2026-11-07): "I also want a calendar picker UI that makes it easy to switch the year, lets you pick with the calendar visible and the day of the week showing in the calendar grid, and the date display should let you type the date in yourself. If we could also show the number of games on each day in that view that would be great."
- No-game days (owner, 2026-11-07, asked whether they are dimmed): "dimmed". They stay pickable.
- Future dates (owner, 2026-11-07, asked whether they are allowed): "allow future".

Where the bound comes from: S123 AC 1 bounds the calendar at the first day BT holds games for. That day is the owner's, from the backfill under the **Every game since 1901** feature (FEATURES.md; owner, 2026-11-07, about the backfill, not the calendar): "This goes all the way back to 1901/04/18."

## Choices made while drawing (to accept or change at ratification)

- *Agent:* mobile opens a bottom sheet (full width, grab handle, 70% Dark scrim); medium and desktop open a 344px popover under the date button with no scrim. The page behind stays drawn so the reader keeps their place.
- *Agent:* the date field sits above the grid, labelled `DATE`, with a muted hint "Type a date, or pick one below."; an invalid date shows "Not a date. Type it like Sep 21, 2026.", a date before the bound shows "Games start Apr 18, 1901. Pick that day or later.", and neither navigates; a valid typed date navigates on Enter or when the field loses focus, never mid-typing.
- *Agent:* the count is a bare number under the day (`15`), muted, with no "games" word, to fit seven columns at 390px; the year view says `391 games` because it has room.
- *Agent:* the count is S124's number for the day as served, which counts every game on that day's schedule, postponed ones and ones not yet final included (S124 AC 1), so a day whose only game was rained out shows `1` and is not dimmed.
- *Agent:* dimming is 35% opacity of the whole day, the same for a day with no games and every day of an off-season month. A day S124 answers `count: null` (it couldn't learn that day's schedule) is drawn like a no-game day: dimmed, no count, pickable, as S124's note and S125 AC 2 expect. A month whose counts are still loading, or whose request failed, dims nothing, since dimming a whole month would read as an off-season month.
- *Agent:* a day, month or year before 1901-04-18 is disabled: the number alone at 20%, no count, not focusable, and the ‹ arrow at the bound disabled the same way; the earliest views add "Games start Apr 18, 1901." under the grid. The 20% and the missing count set it apart from a 35% dimmed day that can still be picked.
- *Agent:* the selected day keeps Primary as its fill (filled red, per the "Red on Dark" decision) with Light text.
- *Agent:* the footer's `Today` goes to the day the scoreboard opens on, the server's answer from `useScoreboardDefaultDate` (`web/src/api/scoreboardDate.ts`, which falls back to `mlbToday` only when the server can't answer), never the device's own clock, so a reader in UTC+9 at 01:00 lands on the scoreboard's day. In the off-season that is the last season's final day, the same day the scoreboard opens on. It then closes. While that day is still loading (`useScoreboardDefaultDate` returns nothing yet), `Today` is drawn in muted text and does nothing. `Close` closes without changing the date (tapping the scrim or pressing Escape does the same). No day is highlighted as today in the grid.
- *Agent:* the drawn counts are illustrative (a 15-game day, 9 or 10 on Mondays and Thursdays, `4` on Wild Card days, 4 to 8 a day in April 1901); real counts come from S124.

## Open questions

1. **Q1.** **Should mobile open the calendar as a bottom sheet while medium and desktop open a popover under the date?** That is how it is drawn: on a phone the sheet rises from the bottom over a dimmed page; on wider screens a small panel drops from the date. The alternative is to use one of the two everywhere.
2. **Q2.** **Is a bare number under each day clear enough to mean "games that day"?** The month grid shows `15` under a day with fifteen games, with no word beside it, to fit seven columns on a phone. Alternatives are `15 g`, or a dot that only says "there are games".
3. **Q3.** **Keep the `Today` and `Close` buttons at the bottom of the calendar?** `Today` jumps back to the scoreboard's day and closes; `Close` closes without changing anything. Without them, tapping outside closes the calendar and the date field can still be typed into.
4. **Q4.** **Keep the previous-day and next-day arrows beside the new date button?** They are drawn as they are today, ‹ and › on either side of the date, so a reader can still step one day at a time without opening the calendar.
5. **Q5.** **Should the year view show each month's game count (`391 games`) and dim months with none?** The year view drawn here puts a total under each month. The game-count service S124 builds answers one month per request, and a current or future month can make it ask MLB for that month's schedule, so the year view would need twelve requests every time it opens, or a new year-total route that no story builds yet. If no, the year view shows month names only, nothing dimmed, and its frames are redrawn. If yes, S124 or a new story adds the year totals before S125 can draw them.
