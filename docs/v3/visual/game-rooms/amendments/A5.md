# A5 — One place for the inning, count and outs on Live

Amends the ratified [game-rooms design](../DESIGN.md) (ratified 2026-10-14) and its amendments [A1](A1.md)–[A4](A4.md). By owner direction, 2026-10-31; built by S109.

**Adds:** nothing.
**Overrides:** the Live status row at every pairing reads only the inning (`Bot 7th`), beside the score and the B / S / O pips; `live-mobile`, `live-medium` and `live-desktop` are redrawn under their own names. A4's `live-hidden-nonotice-mobile` and `live-hidden-nonotice-medium` are superseded by `live-hidden-status-mobile` and `live-hidden-status-medium`, with A4's files left sealed in its manifest.
**Repeals:** the outs after the inning (`Bot 7th · 1 Out`), and the line under the status row on mobile, `Count: 2-2 · 1 Out`.

## Decisions

Recorded as the owner stated them. On 2026-10-31, after the S19 design match, the owner said: "They have the compact version of the like Menon base ball strikes outs, tips, and stuff, which I like. I wanna keep that thing, but on the right side of that it says the inning, and then it says the number of outs again, even though that’s in the pips. Then under that, it says the account and the number of outs again. So I wanna remove the number of outs on the right side of the compacted status thing where it’s next to the inning, and then I wanna remove the number of outs and the count that’s in the UI right under that. That way we will have one representation for the inning and the count and the outs, and it’ll all be in that one row".

- The compact score / bases / B-S-O row stays as drawn.
- The inning shows once, in that row, with no outs after it.
- The count and outs show only as the pips: no `Count: … · … Out` line.
