# A6 — The Plays room opened on a video's play, and a video line on every play with a clip (S96)

Amends the ratified [game-rooms design](../DESIGN.md) (ratified 2026-10-14) and its amendments [A1](A1.md)–[A5](A5.md). Story **S96**; built by S97.

**Adds:** the Plays room as a video's play link opens it: the play's inning chosen in the sidebar and its at-bat open, marked by a 3px bar in the link color down its left edge. Above every at-bat that has a video, open or not and however the room was reached, a line in the link color reads `Video: <clip title>`.
**Overrides:** nothing.
**Repeals:** nothing.

Source: https://www.figma.com/design/wqGKEFlvyxq0ObWZhRJdPF — section "V — Video play links (S96, awaiting ratification)", live file as edited in the S96 session (no named version).

## Decisions

Recorded as the owner stated them. On 2026-10-30 the owner said: "The ones with plays associated should havea link to the play in question. And yes, we should associate the ABS ones." Shown the drafts on 2026-11-04, the owner said: "The “from the video” line should instead say “video:” then the video title. It should show this for any play that has a video, even if you didn’t get there from the video", and, asked whether to keep the bar on the at-bat the link opened: "Keep".

- The link opens the Plays room on that play: its inning is chosen and its at-bat is open (S96 AC 1).
- Every at-bat with a video shows `Video: <clip title>` above it, whether or not the viewer came from that video.
- The opened at-bat keeps the bar down its left edge.

## Choices made while drawing (accepted with the ratification)

- The video line and the bar use the link color, so no new color is introduced.
- The rest of the inning stays as the room draws it, so the opened at-bat sits among its neighbors.
- Whether the bar clears when the viewer opens a different at-bat or inning was asked and not answered; the frames draw only the room as it opens.
