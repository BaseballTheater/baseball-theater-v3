## Decisions

Recorded as the owner stated them. On 2026-10-30 the owner said: "The ones with plays associated should havea link to the play in question. And yes, we should associate the ABS ones."

- The link opens the Plays room on that play, not just the room: its inning is chosen and its at-bat is open (S96 AC 1).

## Choices made while drawing (to accept or change at ratification)

- The mark is a line of text above the at-bat plus a left bar, both in the link color, so the play reads as "where you came from" without a new color.
- The rest of the inning stays as the room draws it, so the opened at-bat sits among its neighbors.
