Amends the ratified [game-shell design](../DESIGN.md) (ratified 2026-10-14) and its amendments [A1](A1.md)–[A4](A4.md). Story **S96**; built by S97. **Draft, not ratified.**

## Decisions

Recorded as the owner stated them. On 2026-10-30 the owner said: "The ones with plays associated should havea link to the play in question. And yes, we should associate the ABS ones."

- A clip whose play is known links to it; a clip with no play shows nothing.
- ABS challenge clips are associated with their pitch's play like any other clip (the `filthy 99 mph backdoor` clip stands in for a pitch-level clip).

## Choices made while drawing (to accept or change at ratification)

- The link reads `Play · <half> <inning> →`, in the link color and type Search's `Go to game` link uses, one size down on cards (11 mobile, 12 medium and desktop).
- It sits on its own line under `Highlight · <date>`, so card titles and meta keep their place.
- In the player it takes the top-left slot A4 gave the game link; inside a game's Videos room there is no game link, so nothing competes for it.
- The medium and desktop copies drop the `Filter` button, as A1 repealed it for launch.
- Desktop frames keep the ratified 1280px grid canvas (DESIGN.md's note on `game-videos-grid-desktop`).
