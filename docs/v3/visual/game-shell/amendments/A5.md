# A5 — A video's link to its play, and no play icon on thumbnails (S96)

Amends the ratified [game-shell design](../DESIGN.md) (ratified 2026-10-14) and its amendments [A1](A1.md)–[A4](A4.md). Story **S96**; built by S97.

**Adds:** a play link on every video card and list row whose clip has a play (`Play · Top 6th →`), and the same link at the top left of the floating player's overlay row. Clips with no play (Condensed Game, Recap) show no link. List mode at medium and desktop, which was only ever drawn at mobile.
**Overrides:** every video thumbnail on this surface, wherever it is drawn (the Videos room in grid and list, every game phase, Hide Scores, and the room behind the player): it has no play icon. The duration badge stays.
**Repeals:** the play icon on video thumbnails.

Source: https://www.figma.com/design/wqGKEFlvyxq0ObWZhRJdPF — section "V — Video play links (S96, awaiting ratification)", live file as edited in the S96 session (no named version).

## Decisions

Recorded as the owner stated them. On 2026-10-30 the owner said: "The ones with plays associated should havea link to the play in question. And yes, we should associate the ABS ones." Shown the drafts on 2026-11-04, the owner said: "Remove the play button on the video thumbnails", and then: "No thumbnails should have a play icon".

- A clip whose play is known links to it; a clip with no play shows nothing.
- ABS challenge clips are associated with their pitch's play like any other clip.
- No video thumbnail has a play icon; S97 removes it from the built `VideoCard` along with adding the link.

## Choices made while drawing (accepted with the ratification)

- The link reads `Play · <half> <inning> →`, in the link color and type Search's `Go to game` link uses, one size down on cards (11 mobile, 12 medium and desktop).
- It sits on its own line under `Highlight · <date>`, so card titles and meta keep their place.
- In the player it takes the top-left slot A4 gave the game link; inside a game's Videos room there is no game link, so nothing competes for it.
- The medium and desktop copies drop the `Filter` button, as A1 repealed it for launch.
- List mode at medium and desktop is the mobile rows at full width.
- Desktop frames keep the ratified 1280px grid canvas (DESIGN.md's note on `game-videos-grid-desktop`).
- Whether Search's floating player (standings-search-settings A6) also gets the play link was asked and not answered; that surface is outside this amendment, so it is unchanged here.
