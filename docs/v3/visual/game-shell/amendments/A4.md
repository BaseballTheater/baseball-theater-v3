# A4 — The video dialog floats over the dimmed page (S70)

Amends the ratified [game-shell design](../DESIGN.md) (ratified 2026-10-14) and its amendments [A1](A1.md)–[A3](A3.md). Story **S70**.

**Adds:** a labeled **Close ✕** button in the dimmed overlay at top right, and the game link (when the clip has one) at top left of the overlay.
**Overrides:** `video-player-dialog-mobile` and `video-player-dialog-medium` — the player has no card: no background, border or corner radius. It floats centered on the scrim, which dims the page fully.
**Repeals:** the dialog card and the bare `✕` in its corner.

## Decisions

Recorded as the owner stated them. On 2026-10-31, shown the S70 build beside the ratified dialog, the owner answered: "I would prefer that the video dialog be more like a floating video with a “Close” button in the dimmed overlay, no borders, so there’s more real estate to show a playlist in the future, etc." and "on mobile, I want to prevent the video from automatically going fullscreeen when it plays on iOS."

- **Floating player:** no card, border or background; the player sits on the scrim.
- **Close:** a labeled "Close" button in the overlay, not inside the player.
- **iOS:** a clip plays inline and never goes fullscreen by itself. This is a behavior, not something drawn.

## Choices made while drawing (accepted with the ratification)

- Close reads "Close ✕", in Light text at full strength, 16px from the top-right corner of the screen.
- The game link moves out of the card to the top left of the overlay, on the same row as Close.
- The player stays the same width as before (mobile: the page width minus 16px each side; medium: 560). The room around it is left free for a future playlist; nothing is drawn there.
- The scrim stays fully opaque Dark, so the title and disclaimer read cleanly without a card behind them.
