**Gap:** A video card whose clip has a play shows the play link on a line of its own under the meta line (`Play · Top 6th →`, game-shell [A5](./visual/game-shell/amendments/A5.md), ratified by S96 and built by S97 in `web/src/components/videos/VideoCard.tsx`), and the meta line reads `Highlight · Sep 5, 2026`. Looking at section V in Figma on 2026-11-10 the owner asked for a change (owner, 2026-11-10: *"Move hte play link to the 2nd line. Replace "Highlight" with "Play" in red. If there's no play, just keep "Highlight""*). A5 is ratified and built, so the change is a new amendment (ratified files are never edited, CLAUDE.md), and S145 builds it.

**Impact:** Before S144, a video of a play had its link to the play on an extra line under the card. S144 impact: the owner approves a card where the second line itself says "Play" in red and takes you to the play, and a clip with no play still says "Highlight".
