**Gap:** The owner wants Standings to show three views (owner, 2026-11-10: *"I think the standings view should have an option. Basically, it should be capable of showing spring training, regular season, and playoffs."*), with rules for which can be chosen on a day and which opens first (FEATURES, Standings row). `GET /api/v1/standings` serves one table per day, the one `standingsPhase` picks (S128; `packages/domain/src/standings-phase.ts`, `functions/src/services/standings.ts`). Nothing says which views a day offers or which opens first, and a fan can't ask for a view: in spring training there is no way to see the regular season's table, and in the playoffs none to see the teams that made it.

**Impact:** Before S139, the standings data was one table chosen by the calendar. S139 impact: the data can answer for spring training, the regular season or the playoffs on any day, and says which of them a fan can choose that day and which opens first, so S130's page can offer the choice. Nothing on the page changes yet.

1. One named, tested function in `packages/domain` (ADR-025), beside `standingsPhase`, answers for a day, the season calendars it is given, and whether any team in that day's regular-season table has clinched a playoff spot: the views a fan can choose that day, in the order `spring`, `regularSeason`, `playoffs`, and the one that opens first. It reads the day and the calendars only, never the clock, so a past date gets the answer it would have had on that day (owner, 2026-11-10: *"Same rules as if that date were today."*). The rules, each from the owner's words of 2026-11-10 quoted in FEATURES' Standings row:
    - **`regularSeason`** can always be chosen. It opens first on every day the two rules below don't name.
    - **`spring`** can be chosen from a season's `springStartDate` through April 15 of that season's year (owner: *"In fact, just do April 15th."*), or through the day before its `regularSeasonStartDate` when that is later (the agent's choice for a season that opens after April 15, as 2020's did; the owner's rule names April 15 only). It opens first from `springStartDate` through the day before `regularSeasonStartDate`.
    - **`playoffs`** can be chosen on a day from a season's `regularSeasonStartDate` through the day before the next known season's `springStartDate` (with no later season known, with no end), when a team has clinched. It opens first from the day after `regularSeasonEndDate` through the sixth day after that season's `lastGameDate`, when it can be chosen; from the seventh day the regular season opens first again and `playoffs` can still be chosen (owner: *"A week after the last playoff game, we should switch back to the regular season by default"*; *"I think we should keep the playoffs view showing."*).
    - Before every known season, or with no calendar, only `regularSeason`.

    Unit tests with the 2026 and 2027 calendars, each day with and without a clinched team where that changes the answer: 2027-02-18 (the off-season's last day), 2027-02-19 (spring opens first; `playoffs` gone), 2027-03-24, 2027-03-25 (the regular season opens first; `spring` still offered), 2027-04-15 and 2027-04-16 (`spring` offered, then not), 2026-09-05, 2026-09-27 (the regular season's last day: it still opens first), 2026-09-28 (`playoffs` opens first; with no clinched team the regular season does), the sixth and seventh days after 2026's `lastGameDate`, a season whose first regular-season game is after April 15, and no calendar.

2. `GET /api/v1/standings` takes `view` beside `date`, and its answer gains `view` (the view the returned table belongs to) and `views: { offered, default }` from AC 1's function. `phase`, `season` and `date` keep S128's meaning and describe the table returned; `phase`'s values don't change.
    - **No `view`:** the answer is the table S128 serves today, unchanged, plus the new fields (`view` is `spring` for a spring table, otherwise `regularSeason`). A client that doesn't know views (the page before S130, an installed app that hasn't updated) keeps getting a full table.
    - **`view=default`:** the day's default view, with S128 AC 3's fallback on the two mornings it covers: the first spring day and opening day answer with the previous phase's table while their own is empty, and `view` names that table's view.
    - **`view=spring`, `regularSeason` or `playoffs`:** that view when the day offers it; when it doesn't, the answer `view=default` gives, so an old or shared link still shows standings and the answer's `view` says which.
    - **Anything else:** 400 `view_invalid`, with a hint naming the four values.

    The tables: `spring` is that spring's table as of the day, or as of the day before `regularSeasonStartDate` once the regular season has started. `regularSeason` is the day's table in a regular season, the season's final table from its end until the next `springStartDate` (S128's `final`), and on a spring day the new season's regular-season table for that day (`phase: "regularSeason"`). `playoffs` is the `regularSeason` view's table for that day keeping only the teams with `clinched`, in the same division order, a division with none left out (the page shows them with no numbers, owner: *"it'll just be a list of teams with no with no numbers"*) (route tests on recorded fixtures: every case above, and `?date=2026-10-05` with each of the four values).

**Notes:**

- **What the owner decided, and where it is quoted.** FEATURES' Standings row holds the owner's words of 2026-11-10 for every rule in AC 1. The owner first described hiding the spring link a week after each team's first game, then replaced it: *"If that's complicated to figure out, then we can pick an arbitrary day like April 15th. In fact, just do April 15th."*
- **What the playoffs view holds later is not decided** (owner, 2026-11-10: *"I don't know what the playoff standings page will actually be yet, because we can't just show like wins and losses because it's per round. So we'll have to figure that out later. That's fine. We'll figure that out later."*). This story serves the list of teams that have clinched and nothing about rounds; no story builds more until the owner says what it shows.
- **Why "no `view`" stays as it is.** From the day after the regular season ends, the default view is the playoffs list. An installed app that hasn't updated draws whatever table it gets as full standings, so it would draw that list as a short table of records. Keeping the unparameterised answer as S128's, and giving the new page `view=default`, avoids that.
- **Clinching comes from MLB.** `StandingsTeamRecord.clinched` is mapped from MLB's `clinched` (`functions/src/mappers/standings.ts`). The final table carries it (`fixtures/raw/standings-2026-final.json`); AC 5 checks that a dated table carries it as of its day. A season whose table marks no team (1994, or an old season MLB doesn't mark) never offers `playoffs`, and its off-season opens on the regular season.
- **Spring's games-behind column** is hidden by the page (S130, owner: *"There, just hide that. Just don't show the column."*); the spring table's data doesn't change here.
- **The World Series winner** for the owner's box is S140's, from the World Series schedule the calendars already read.
- `web/src/api/generated.types.test.ts` lists each response type and `pnpm verify`'s drift gate (`web/src/api/codegen.test.ts`) re-renders `web/src/api/generated/` from the spec.
