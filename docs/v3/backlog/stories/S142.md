Today three pages each draw their own one-line footer (`FooterMeta` in `web/src/layout/FooterMeta.tsx`, called by `ScoreboardPage.tsx`, `GamePage.tsx` and `StandingsPage.tsx`), the other pages have none, and there is no privacy policy, user agreement, about or contact page.

**Impact:** Before S142, the site had no footer to speak of, just a "Fetched … ago" line on three pages. S142 impact: every page ends in the same footer of links the owner approved, and the "Fetched … ago" line is gone.
