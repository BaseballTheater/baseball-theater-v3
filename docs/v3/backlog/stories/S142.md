**Gap:** The owner wants every page to end in a footer with a few columns of links (owner, 2026-11-10: *"we need probably a footer like banner kind of section-y thing like most places have where there's a few columns of links and stuff"*) and the "Fetched … ago" line gone (*"I don't think we need the fetched thing at all"*). Today three pages each draw their own one-line footer (`FooterMeta` in `web/src/layout/FooterMeta.tsx`, called by `ScoreboardPage.tsx`, `GamePage.tsx` and `StandingsPage.tsx`), the other pages have none, and there is no privacy policy, user agreement, about or contact page.

**Impact:** Before S142, the site had no footer to speak of: no copyright line, no policies, no way to find who made it or to get in touch. S142 impact: every page ends in the same footer, with links to the privacy policy, the user agreement, the fan's own data, an about page and a way to make contact.
