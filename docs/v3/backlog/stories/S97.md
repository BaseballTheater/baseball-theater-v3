**Acceptance criteria** (checks `scripts/verify-S97.sh` performs)

1. A video with a `playId` shows a link reading `Play · <half> <inning> →` (e.g. `Play · Top 6th →`, `Play · Bot 8th →`) on its own line under its `Highlight · <date>` meta line, in grid and list layout (game-shell A5); one without a `playId` (e.g. Condensed Game, Recap) shows none. RTL on 823823: 20 links, the exact text for one known clip, and choosing the link navigates without opening the player.
2. The floating player shows the same play link at top left of its overlay row when its clip has a `playId` and no game link already holds that slot (game-shell A5 `video-player-dialog-playlink-*`); choosing it closes the player and opens the play (RTL).
3. No video thumbnail in the Videos room shows a play icon — grid and list, with scores shown and hidden — and every still keeps its duration badge (game-shell A5). RTL: no `IconPlayerPlay` / play-mark element inside any `VideoCard`, duration text present.
4. The link opens `/game/:gamePk/plays?play=<playId>`; the Plays room chooses that play's inning, opens its at-bat, marks it with the 3px link-color bar down its left edge (game-rooms A6), and scrolls it into view (RTL); an unknown `play` opens the room as usual.
5. The Plays room shows `Video: <clip title>` in the link color above every at-bat one of whose pitches a video's `playId` names, open or closed, whether or not `?play=` is present (game-rooms A6). RTL on 823823: the number of lines equals the number of distinct at-bats the 20 linked clips fall in, a known at-bat shows its clip's title, and an at-bat with no clip shows none.
6. Design match (rule 22): for each of these 11 ratified frames — game-shell A5 `game-videos-grid-playlink-{mobile,medium,desktop}`, `game-videos-list-playlink-{mobile,medium,desktop}`, `video-player-dialog-playlink-{mobile,medium}`; game-rooms A6 `plays-opened-{mobile,medium,desktop}` (desktop captured at 1200px, owner 2026-10-28: *"Capture at 1200px"*, though the frames keep the 1280px canvas) — `pnpm design:capture S97` saves the built page to `docs/v3/design-checks/S97/<frame>.png` from `e2e/design/S97.spec.ts`; the grader checks each of the 11 files exists at its pairing's width. **Notes (2026-11-04 design intake review, at `ac18059`; superseded where the next note says so):**

- **The player is inside this fence.** `PlayerDialog.tsx`, `PlayerStage.tsx`, `PlayerHost.tsx` and `LazyPlayerDialog.tsx` are all in `web/src/components/videos/` at `ac18059`, which answers the 2026-10-30 note above.
- **A video's `playId` is a pitch's, not an at-bat's.** On 823823, 20 of 40 highlights carry a `playId`, all 20 distinct, and every one matches a `PitchEvent.playId` in `plays-823823.json`; none is an at-bat id. Distinct pitches can share an at-bat, so 20 card links need not mean 20 `Video:` lines (AC 5 counts distinct at-bats).
- **The link's `Top 6th` label.** `inningLabel` (`packages/domain/src/inning.ts:31`) makes `Top 6th` / `Bot 6th` from a linescore, not an at-bat, and `PlaysPanel.tsx` spells halves inline (`Top`/`Bottom` at :34, `inningTag` `Top 3`/`Bot 7` at :52, section labels `Bottom 6th · NYY` at :203). The frames spell the bottom half `Bot` with an ordinal. Give the at-bat form one domain function, reusing `ordinal`, rather than another inline spelling.

**Notes (2026-11-04 design intake review after S96 ratified game-shell A5 and game-rooms A6, at `58aa31d`):**

- **Ratified; the ACs now carry the frames.** The earlier intake's premise that A6 marks the at-bat `From the video · <clip title>` is superseded: the ratified A6 marks the opened at-bat with a 3px link-color bar and puts `Video: <clip title>` above every at-bat with a clip, however the room was reached. So the URL needs only `?play=<playId>` — no clip param. The `Video:` line is drawn as link-colored text, not a control; don't make it a link.
- **One lookup.** `atBatIndexByPitch` is already exported (`packages/domain/src/video-play-links.ts:23`; the earlier note's "private `atBatOfPitch`" is stale — that is its local name inside `videoPlayLinks`). Reuse it for the card/player label, the `?play=` arrival and the `Video:` lines (ADR-025). `PlaysPanel` gets `game: GamePayload`, whose `highlights` already carry each clip's `playId` — no new fetch.
- **The card is one button.** `VideoCard` renders the whole card as an `UnstyledButton` (`aria-label="Play <title>"`, `onClick={() => onPlay(video)}`); a router link inside it would be nested interactive content and would also start the clip. Restructure the card (button for still/title, sibling link) rather than nesting. Keep the `aria-label` — it is text, not the icon AC 3 removes.
- **The play icon lives in `VideoStill`.** `VideoStill` (`VideoCard.tsx`) draws the centered `IconPlayerPlay` unless `bare`; only `VideoCard` uses it non-bare. Remove the icon branch there and keep the duration badge — `bare` (used by `DayDigest`) also drops the duration, so it is not the fix. Update `VideoStill`'s and `OVERLAY`'s comments in the same change (ADR-025).
- **The player's link slot.** `PlayerDialog` shows a game link at top left only when the clip's game is not the one the player was opened from; A5 leaves that slot as it is on other surfaces (Scoreboard digest, Search). AC 2's link fills it only when it is empty. The dialog gets `video` and `game`, not plays, so its label needs the at-bat for the `playId` passed in or looked up.
- **List mode** already renders at every pairing (`VideosRoom.tsx`, `data-testid="videos-list"`); the medium/desktop list frames may need only captures.
- **Not built here (owner questions, 2026-11-04 intake):** whether the arrival bar clears when another at-bat or inning is opened (until answered, build only the arrival state); one `Video:` line per clip or per at-bat when an at-bat has several clips (until answered, one line per at-bat as drawn); whether the play link shows with Hide Scores on (no hidden frame draws it); Search's floating player (out of A5 and this fence — a separate amendment and story if wanted).
