**Gap:** The owner wants a footer like most sites have, with a few columns of links (owner, 2026-11-10: *"we need probably a footer like banner kind of section-y thing like most places have where there's a few columns of links and stuff"*), holding a copyright line, the policies, a link to the fan's data, an about page and a way to make contact, and wants the "Fetched … ago" line gone (*"I don't think we need the fetched thing at all"*; *"But I think the footer needs to change quite a bit."*). The ratified footers are one muted line, "Fetched {age} · baseball.theater" (game-shell A3, game-rooms A4), and none of the pages the owner named exists or is drawn, so S142 can't build them without designing in code (rule 18).

**Impact:** Before S141, nothing showed what the site's footer or its policy, about and contact pages look like, and nobody had said what those pages hold. S141 impact: the owner approves the footer, the pages it links to and what each one says before S142 builds them.

**Acceptance criteria** (checks `scripts/verify-S141.sh` performs)

1. A ratified amendment draws each of these at mobile, medium and desktop, from the owner's answers of 2026-11-10 (quoted here and in FEATURES' Site footer row):
    - **The footer.** Its columns, each column's heading and each link's words, and the copyright line with the year (owner: *"The footer needs to have like a copyright and the year and probably a privacy policy"*; *"Like the user agreement or something."*; *"There should always also be a like a my data or delete my data or whatever that common link is down there."*; *"And about uh, and the contact."*). It has room for how the site is made (owner: *"I'll probably want to put some like information on it about the technology and the um, I don't know, the API usage and how I went about building it, and also like I don't know, some other stuff."*). The amendment says how the columns fold at mobile width.
    - **Where it shows.** Every kind of page the shell draws: the scoreboard, a game page with its rooms, Standings, search, Settings and the change log. The amendment says whether it shows in the installed app (ADR-023), and where it sits on a page shorter than the screen.
    - **What goes.** The "Fetched … ago" line. That it goes from every page, not only the change log page the owner was asked about, is the agent's reading of *"at all"*; the owner confirms it when the draft is shown. The amendment says what happens to the rest of what the ratified footers carry: the "baseball.theater" mark, the recap's "Source: MLB" (game-rooms A2), and the offline footer that names the saved copy's time and age (shell-scoreboard A1, game-rooms A1), whose banner already says both.
    - **The pages it links to.** One layout for a page of plain text (its heading, its sections and the date it was last changed), drawn once and used by the privacy policy, the user agreement, the about page and the page on how the site is made, with the address of each. What contact is (an email link, a form or a link elsewhere). Where the link to the fan's data goes for a signed-in fan (Settings already holds the data section with its delete, S79) and for a signed-out visitor.
2. What each page says is the owner's. The amendment's `## Decisions` cites the owner's answer, quoted, to each of these, asked in the session: who writes the privacy policy and the user agreement, and that the owner approved the text that will ship; what the about page says; how a fan makes contact; what the page on how the site is made covers; and the name on the copyright line. FEATURES' Site footer row records the answers in the owner's words in the same change set. The text of each page the owner supplied or approved is committed with the amendment, one file per page beside it in the surface's `amendments/` folder, and `## Decisions` names each file, so S142 has one source to copy and its grader one to compare against (the grader checks each named file exists and isn't empty). A policy's text is never written into the product as approved without the owner's words saying so.
3. The owner ratified it in the session it was shown (quoted), recorded the way the other amendments are.
4. This file's S141 **Status** (story heading and top table) is `done`.

**Notes:**

- A ratified file is never edited (CLAUDE.md), so the story writes only amendments. The footer is the shell's, so its amendment belongs to shell-scoreboard; the other three surfaces' `amendments/` are in the fence because their ratified frames draw the old footer line, and the amendment says whether it replaces that line on their frames by itself or each surface gets its own.
- **The policies are legal text.** The agent may draft them for the owner to read, but whether a draft is good enough to publish, or someone else writes them, is the owner's call (AC 2). Until the owner approves the text, S142 parks rather than ship a placeholder that reads as a policy.
- Deleting your data is already built (S79, in Settings); this story only draws the link to it.
- S133's change log frames draw no "Fetched … ago" line and no site footer; they gain this footer when S142 builds it for every page.
- S74 (offline, not built yet) builds the offline banner and footer from shell-scoreboard A1; if this story changes that footer, S74's note gains the change in the same change set.
- **2026-11-10 backlog review.** Three things the ACs left open:
    - **The approved text had no home.** S142 AC 4's grader compares each page's text with "the text S141's amendment names", but nothing here said the text is committed anywhere, so an amendment that only quoted the owner's "approved" would pass this story and leave S142 nothing to check against. AC 2 now has the text committed beside the amendment.
    - **A contact form is not in S142.** AC 1 lets contact be "an email link, a form or a link elsewhere". An email link or a link elsewhere fits S142 (its fence is the web app). A form needs somewhere to send what a fan types: an API route, a store, and an entry in the personal-data inventory (ADR-028), since a message carries an email address. If the owner chooses a form, this story's change set slices that as its own story and S142 builds only the page.
    - **What a privacy policy draft is drafted from.** What BT stores about a person is listed in the personal-data inventory (`packages/ports/src/personal-data.ts`, `functions/src/services/personal-data.ts`; ADR-028). A draft takes its list of stored data from there, so the policy and the product don't disagree.
- **If someone else writes the policies later**, AC 2 keeps this story open until their text is approved, and S142, the whole footer, waits with it. Whether the footer may ship first without the two policy links is the owner's to say; asked with the footer's other questions.

**Reuse check** (ADR-025): before drawing, read the ratified footers (game-shell A3, game-rooms A2 and A4), the offline frames' footer (shell-scoreboard A1, game-rooms A1), the Settings frames' data section (standings-search-settings), and VISUAL-DESIGN's link, heading and muted-text tokens; a design story writes no code; record what was reused, or why nothing fit, in the story's ledger notes.
