**Gap:** The ratified scoreboard frames draw the Win Probability slot occupant only on a scheduled game, before first pitch (`shell-scoreboard/DESIGN.md`: "win probability (scheduled row)"), and Settings lists it as a Game-Row Slot choice. No feed BT has found gives pre-game odds, and the owner wants it where it is useful (owner, 2026-10-30, told that the frames draw it only before first pitch and no feed gives pre-game odds: *"It is actually least useful before the game. During and after is definitely useful."*). Nothing ratified draws it on a live or final row, or says what a scheduled row shows with it chosen, or how it looks with Hide Scores on.

**Impact:** Before S107, building the win-probability slot would mean designing it in code. S107 impact: the owner approves how a game's win odds look on the scoreboard while it is live and after it ends, before S108 builds them.
