**Gap:** The owner asked that the update notice link to the change log page, that a notification after updating link to it and dismiss itself, and that really big additions show on both (owner, 2026-11-08: *"The update dialog should link to it and a notification that auto dismisses after updating should show a link to it. Really big new additions should show on the update notification itself (via API data comparing versions)"*; asked which notification, *"Both"*). On S133's first draft the owner took the big additions off the after-update notification and ruled out two boxes at once (owner, 2026-11-09, shown the A9 draft, where image 1 showed the after-update notification stacked above the update notice, each a message with "See what's new" and its buttons and neither listing additions, and image 2 the after-update notification listing the big additions: *"Image 1 here looks good. Image 2 is weird, no need to show that"*; asked which notices should stop listing them: *"There shouldn’t be two boxes ever. After it updates, we should see something similar to image 1, but it should tell the user the the update is complete instead"*). On the second round the owner cut the update notice to one big addition and replaced the after-update box with a small pointer at the menu's change log link (owner, 2026-11-10; quoted in ACs 2 and 3 and in FEATURES' Update / change log UX row). Today the update notice (S73, A6) says only that an update is ready, nothing shows after updating, and the web app doesn't know which release it is.

**Impact:** Before S135, a fan updated without knowing what they'd get, and saw nothing afterwards. S135 impact: the update notice links to the change log, and after updating a small pointer at the menu says the update is complete and shows where the change log is, then goes.
