**Gap:** The owner asked for a change log page, a link to it from the update notice, a notification after updating that dismisses itself and links to it, and the big additions shown on both notifications (owner, 2026-11-08: *"The update dialog should link to it and a notification that auto dismisses after updating should show a link to it. Really big new additions should show on the update notification itself"*; asked which notification, *"Both"*). The ratified shell draws only the update-available notice (A6), with no link or notes, and nothing draws the page or what shows after updating, so S134 and S135 can't build them without designing in code (rule 18). On the first draft the owner took the big additions off the after-update notification and ruled out two boxes at once (owner, 2026-11-09, shown the A9 draft, where image 1 showed the after-update notification stacked above the update notice, each a message with "See what's new" and its buttons and neither listing additions, and image 2 the after-update notification listing the big additions: *"Image 1 here looks good. Image 2 is weird, no need to show that"*; asked which notices should stop listing them: *"There shouldn’t be two boxes ever. After it updates, we should see something similar to image 1, but it should tell the user the the update is complete instead"*). On the second round the owner named the page the change log, put a link to it at the foot of the menu, replaced the after-update box with a small pointer at that link, and cut the update notice to one big addition (owner, 2026-11-10; quoted in AC 1, the note below and FEATURES' Update / change log UX row).

**Impact:** Before S133, nothing showed what the change log page or the update notices' new parts look like. S133 impact: the owner approves the page, its link in the menu, the update notice with its link and one big addition, and the pointer that shows after updating, before S134 and S135 build them.
