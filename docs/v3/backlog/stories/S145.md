**Gap:** `VideoCard` (`web/src/components/videos/VideoCard.tsx`) draws a clip's play link (`Play · Top 6th →`, S97) as its own line under the meta line, outside the card's button, and the meta line's first word comes from `KIND_LABELS` (`Highlight` for a highlight). The owner wants the link on the meta line instead, as a red "Play" (owner, 2026-11-10: *"Move hte play link to the 2nd line. Replace "Highlight" with "Play" in red. If there's no play, just keep "Highlight""*), drawn by S144.

**Impact:** Before S145, a video of a play had an extra line under it linking to the play. S145 impact: the card's second line says "Play" in red and takes you to the play; a clip with no play still says "Highlight".
