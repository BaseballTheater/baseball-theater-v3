**Gap:** The ratified Live frames at medium and desktop (`game-rooms/live-medium.png`, `live-desktop.png`) draw a **Win probability** card under the status card: the home team's chance of winning over the innings, a dashed 50% line, and the current value. It was left out at launch until the owner picked a data source (owner, 2026-10-19; A2: "Leave it for now", 2026-10-21), so S64 built the room without it. The owner now wants it back (owner, 2026-10-30: *"Bring back win probability charts"*), and S93 gives each at-bat's win probability from MLB's play metrics, the same values Savant shows (85 of 85 at-bats on 823823). With Hide Scores on, the card reads "–" and "Hidden with scores" (game-rooms A3 `live-hidden-medium.png`; A4 `live-hidden-nonotice-medium.png` without the footer notice).

**Impact:** Before S106, the Live room showed the bases, the count and every pitch but not who was likely to win. S106 impact: on a tablet or computer, a chart beside the field shows how each at-bat swung the home team's chances, and where they stand now.
