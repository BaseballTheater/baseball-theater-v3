**Gap:** Once S107 draws it, the Game-Row Slot setting has no Win Probability choice and the scoreboard's game summaries carry no win probability (owner, 2026-10-30, told that the frames draw it only before first pitch and no feed gives pre-game odds: *"It is actually least useful before the game. During and after is definitely useful."*).

**Impact:** Before S108, the scoreboard couldn't show who is likely to win. S108 impact: fans who choose Win Probability for the slot see each live game's odds as they stand, and each finished game's final swing, right on the scoreboard.
