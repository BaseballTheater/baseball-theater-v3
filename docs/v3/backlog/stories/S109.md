**Gap:** The Live room says the outs three times and the count twice: the status row reads `Bot 7th · 1 Out` beside the B / S / O pips (`LiveStatus.tsx` joins `outsLabel` onto `inningLabel`), and on mobile the matchup line adds `Count: 2-2 · 1 Out` (`LiveRoom.tsx`). The owner, 2026-10-31, after S19's design match: *"So I wanna remove the number of outs on the right side of the compacted status thing where it’s next to the inning, and then I wanna remove the number of outs and the count that’s in the UI right under that. That way we will have one representation for the inning and the count and the outs, and it’ll all be in that one row"*. The owner ratified `game-rooms/amendments/A5.md` on 2026-11-01 (*"Signed off"*).

**Impact:** Before S109, the Live page repeated the outs and the count in words right beside the dots that already show them. S109 impact: the inning shows once, as "Bot 7th", and the count and outs show only as the dots in that one row, so there is less to read.
