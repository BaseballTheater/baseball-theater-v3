**Gap:** The app always opens on 2024-07-04, the fixture day hard-coded in `web/src/routes.tsx` (`/` and `/games` redirect there) and `web/src/pages/ScoreboardPage.tsx` (the default `date`). Nothing knows today's date or the season's calendar. The owner, 2026-11-02, after asking when the work to fetch real game data happens: *"Yes, add a story and make it the top priority. Show the final day of the season by default, rather than \"today\" if today is between the final day of the season and the next spring training first game."* Asked what "the final day of the season" means, the owner chose *"Last postseason game"*. Added by the owner (2026-11-02).

**Impact:** Before S111, opening Baseball Theater always showed a game day from 2024. After it, the scoreboard opens on today's games, and between the World Series and the first spring training game it opens on the World Series final, so there is always something to look at.
