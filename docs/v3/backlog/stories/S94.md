**Gap:** The content feed BT reads gives a video's play only through `guid`, and ABS challenge clips have none. MLB's GraphQL game content (`data-graph.mlb.com`, `getGamesByGamePks`) gives `playIdRef` on 18 of 823823's 25 videos — three of the five ABS clips among them — plus the recap and related articles (owner, 2026-10-30: *"Also add stories to ingest all of this extra data."*).

**Impact:** Before S94, BT had no way to tell which play an ABS challenge video shows. S94 impact: BT keeps MLB's own play link for those videos, and the game's related articles, so the next stories can connect videos to plays.

- **What the endpoint needs (found while preparing, `3e3a4a2`; read-only probe).** The full persisted-query hash is `e12a5c2692406844d5f15f52ce7754208f53da81867de7d3e9e773611f4a040a`. `data-graph.mlb.com` answers **400 with an empty body** unless the request carries the header `x-client-name: mlb`. The variables also take `gameRecapTags: ["game-recap"]`, `relatedArticleTags: ["storytype-article"]` and `contentSource: "MLB"` besides `gamePks` and `locale`. On 823823 the response has 25 videos, one `recapArticle` and `relatedArticles`. Keep the hash, header and variables in one URL/request builder in `functions/src/adapters/http/mlb.ts` (as S93's `playMetricsUrl` is for its host), which the recorder reuses, and test that the header is sent.
- **Articles carry a slug, not a URL.** AC 4's "URL" is read as the article's slug, the only link MLB's response gives; whoever shows an article builds the mlb.com link from it. No story reads `relatedArticles` yet, so nothing else depends on the form.
- **Refreshes with the game.** S93's `fetchGame` attaches play metrics on every fetch (`Promise.all` at `functions/src/adapters/http/mlb.ts:101`); attach the GraphQL content the same way, best-effort (null on failure, never a throw), so AC 5 holds and live ingest keeps it current.
- **The GraphQL helper is S94's alone for now.** ADR-029 and S5's notes say one GraphQL helper serves both `fastball-gateway` (S5) and `data-graph` (S94). S5 is parked on an owner question about what its search can ask MLB, and this endpoint is a persisted-query request with a required header, so build only what S94 needs; S5 reuses or extends it if it goes ahead.
