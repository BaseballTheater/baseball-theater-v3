**Gap:** `MediaHighlight.playId` is filled from `mediaPlaybackId`, the video file's id, so no video matches a play (0 of 40 on 823823). MLB's `guid` links 15 of them, S94's `playIdRef` adds three ABS clips, and S93's ABS-challenge list leaves the other two to one challenge each (owner, 2026-10-30: *"The ones with plays associated should havea link to the play in question. And yes, we should associate the ABS ones."*).

**Impact:** Before S95, BT didn't know which play a video shows. S95 impact: every video of a single play — including the ABS challenge clips — knows its play, so the app can link the two.
