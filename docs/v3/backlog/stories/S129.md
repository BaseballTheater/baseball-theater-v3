**Gap:** The owner wants Standings to show spring training, the regular season or the playoffs, one at a time, with the fan able to switch (owner, 2026-11-10: *"I think the standings view should have an option. Basically, it should be capable of showing spring training, regular season, and playoffs."*), a line saying which day's standings these are when it isn't today, a box naming the World Series winner in the off-season, and a page title that says what is shown (FEATURES, Standings row). The ratified Standings frames (standings-search-settings, A7) draw only the regular-season tables, so nothing says how the views are chosen or what each looks like, and S130 can't build them without designing in code (rule 18). This story began as the label for spring training's standings (owner, 2026-11-07, asked what to show from the first spring game until opening day, with spring training standings, labeled as such, suggested: *"Agreed"*); the owner's answers about its draft turned the label into the views.

**Impact:** Before S129, nothing showed how a fan tells spring training's standings from the real ones, or gets from one to the other. S129 impact: the owner approves how Standings offers its three views, the "Standings as of" line and the World Series winner's box before S130 builds them.
